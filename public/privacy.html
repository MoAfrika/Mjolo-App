Your privacy. Your pace.

Mjolo is for adults aged 18 and over. You must provide an accurate birth date and confirm adult eligibility. Email verification confirms control of an email address; it is not identity verification, an age-document check, or a background check.

What the app stores

Your authentication provider stores your email and password securely. The app stores your profile, private birth date, interests and preferences, photo files, likes, matches, messages, date plans, safety reports, and a limited administrative audit trail. Passwords and privileged keys are never included in public profiles. Do not put contact information or home addresses in a public profile.

Who can see it

Eligible members may see the profile details you choose to share. Your birth date and email are not shown. You can hide your city and broad area, or pause discovery. Private photos require an authorised session. Existing matches retain access to your profile when discovery is paused. Messages and date plans are restricted to active, unblocked match participants. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorised service operators can access infrastructure data to operate the service and investigate issues.

Your choices

Use You to edit your profile, pause discovery, change privacy settings, export your data, change your password, or delete your account. Blocking removes the match and its messages and plans and prevents further interaction. Unblocking does not restore them. A report remains in the moderation queue until reviewed. Account deletion removes auth, profile, photos, likes, matches and messages from active systems; reports may remain with identifiers removed. Provider backups have their own expiry schedule.

Safety and community rules

Be honest, respectful, and at least 18. Harassment, scams, impersonation, exploitation, and unlawful content are not permitted. Report unsafe behaviour using the flag button. Moderators may suspend reported accounts. Meet at a public venue, arrange your own transport, and share plans only with people you trust.

Date Check-In is optional user-initiated sharing and calendar reminders. The service does not monitor dates, contact emergency services, or guarantee safety. For immediate danger contact local emergency services directly.

Cookies, local storage and third parties

Supabase authentication uses browser storage for sessions. Theme preferences are stored on your device. Supabase processes authentication, database, media and realtime data; Netlify delivers the app and server functions. There are no advertising trackers or compulsory GPS features. Using the app contacts these infrastructure providers.

Before public launch

This notice describes the implementation; it is not a final operator privacy policy. The operator must publish its legal identity, Information Officer and privacy contact, lawful processing basis, retention schedule, hosting region and cross-border transfer safeguards, complaint and appeal process, age-assurance policy, and deletion/backup timelines before accepting real members. Complete a POPIA and security review first. No live privacy contact is configured in this source release.
